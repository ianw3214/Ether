# Ether 

Project **Ether** is a sushi themed autobattler game with farming/base building and gacha mechanics. The primary focus will be a singleplayer campaign/story mode, with potential leaderboards and co-op/PvP modes for multiplayer interactions.

## Design Pillars

- **Simplicity**: Use simple and straightforward systems for major gameplay mechanics, and rely on system interactions for complexity in emergent behaviour.

- **Rewards**: Every action in the game should be rewarding, giving the player a feeling of progression at all times.

- **Flexibility**: The player can choose how much time they want to spend playing - they should not miss out on much for playing a short period of time, and they should have things to do as well if they choose to grind.

## Gameplay & Mechanics

The game is separated into 2 main loops: combat and base-building; These 2 loops should flow simultaniously to allow the player to easily jump between 2 different types of gameplay.

### Combat

The game features idle combat, meaning it requires no additional input from the player. Each combat encounter features a certain amount of units from both the player and the opponent, and the units automatically attack units on the other team based on a certain predetermined priority. The winning team is the one who remains standing after every unit on the opposing team has been defeated (last all of their health).

Each unit has a basic attack, which they will perform on an interval based on their attack speed. Additionally, each unit has a special attack that can be triggered after building up enough mana. A unit can build up mana by performaing an attack or recieving damage - with the amount gained proportional to the damage being recieved.

#### Story Campaign

The story campaign is a series of encounters that the player has to progress through linearly. Each encounter has a predetermined set of enemies for the player to fight, and clearing the encounter rewards the player with resources to upgrade and expand their base. 

#### Unit Upgrades

Fundamentally, units can be leveled up either directly through combat experience or by feeding them harvested plants. Leveling up provides very basic attribute increases that generally boost the power level of a unit.

Additionally, the player can equip items onto their units to further boost specific stats and attributes. These items are also harvested via plants, and require much more time and resources than the basic level-up plants.

### Base Building

Base building allows the player to generate resources which is then used to upgrade their units. The main form element of base building is building farm tiles, which the player can grow various plants on. Additionally, the player can build various buildings to improve the efficiency of their farming or for aesthetics. This can also be expanded for other various utliity buildings for more future gameplay systems.

### Gacha

The gacha system is how the player can draw for more units, and prestige their existing units if they have already been acquired. There are NO plans for microtransactions in the game since it is a singleplayer game, so the game should be generous with gacha draws and probabilities.

## Story & World

The setting/world of the game is sushi themed - with sushi characters and restaurant-themed environments. The main conflict of the game is driven by the divide of popular sushi vs unpopular sushi, and the many differences that arise based on this popularity divide. Popular sushi often hold more power, and are often in charge of the sushi world - causing a lot of disatisfaction with the unpopular sushi.

The story starts with the unpopular sushi starting to band together to take back control of the sushi kingdom. Many are unsatisfied with the current distribution of power and how they are treated, and are seeking more active solutions to improve their own lives. The actual story campaign will start on a more individual level, focusing on a singular unpopular sushi and their journey into this conflict.
